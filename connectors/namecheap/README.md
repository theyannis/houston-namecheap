# Namecheap

Connect agents to your Namecheap account to keep track of your domains and
their DNS.

## Keep an eye on your domains

- List your domains with their expiry dates, auto-renew and lock status.
- Find domains that are expiring soon or have expired.
- See a domain's nameservers, privacy protection and DNS settings.
- Check whether domain names are available to register.

## Manage DNS records

- Read every DNS record of a domain that uses Namecheap DNS.
- With **Write** access, agents can add, update and
  remove records, for example to point a subdomain at a new host or add a
  verification TXT record. Every other record is kept, and each change is read
  back to confirm it was saved.
- Mail settings are protected: a domain using Namecheap email forwarding,
  Private Email or Gmail keeps its mail setting unless the change asks to
  switch it.

Agents can't register, renew or transfer domains, so nothing is ever charged to
your Namecheap balance.
